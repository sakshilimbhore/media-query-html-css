Discover the Beauty of Nagpur

Nagpur is one of Maharashtra's major cities and is popularly known as the Orange City. It is famous for its oranges, historical landmarks, lakes, gardens and vibrant culture.

Whether you love history, nature, food or photography, Nagpur has something interesting to offer.
